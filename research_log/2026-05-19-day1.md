### Gemma 4 E2B-IT architecture (probed 12:50)

| Field | Value | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| `text_config.hidden_size` | **1536** | AR `Linear(d, d)` is 1536² ≈ 2.4M params per layer |
| `text_config.num_hidden_layers` | **35** | Revises plan from 26-layer assumption. Anchor layers become `{3, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 29, 34}` (0-indexed, ~every 4-5 layers). |
| `text_config.num_attention_heads` | 8 | head_dim 256 (unusually wide heads) |
| `text_config.intermediate_size` | 6144 | 4× hidden, standard ratio |
| `text_config.vocab_size` | **262,144** | Massive vocab. Our +262 stroke tokens are noise on top. |
| `tie_word_embeddings` | **True** | Embedding rows and lm_head rows are the SAME tensor. Simpler: train one set, not two. |
| `vision_config.hidden_size` | 768 | Vision encoder is independent; project tokens into LLM space via existing adapter. |
| `vision_config.patch_size` | 16 | 224×224 image → 14×14 = 196 image patches |
| `vision_config.num_hidden_layers` | 16 | ViT-style encoder, 16 deep |
| Processor class | `Gemma4Processor` | Multimodal preprocessor available. `<|image>` special token in tokenizer. |

**Decision**: anchor layers updated to `{3, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 29, 34}` (0-indexed, 35 total). Final-layer entry is ℓ=34 (the last residual stream). For per-layer-conditioned strategy we'd condition on ℓ∈{0..34}; for full per-layer (Option A) we train 8 independent pairs.

## Decisions made

- **Architecture**: NLA-style autoencoder with stroke output, AR uses Gemma 4's vision pathway. Decided in conversation.
- **Base model**: Gemma 4 E2B (smallest open variant, multimodal-input).
- **Stroke representation**: Cartesian stroke-5 with 128-bin quantisation of Δx, Δy (not polar — Cursive Transformer's choice was an outlier; Cartesian generalises better).
- **Layer-sharing strategy**: full per-layer (Option A) is the gold standard. Train 8 independent (AVℓ, ARℓ) pairs. Anthropic's released NLAs are also per-layer.
- **Anchor layers**: default `{3, 6, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 26}`, overridden by Day-0 ROI ranking.
- **Repo**: PUBLIC from day 1, building in public.
- **Backup**: Mac mirrors everything important. GCP holds raw datasets. No HF Hub.

### What works
- Stage 1 SFT trained AV in 103 s on 1× H200 → AV emits real stroke tokens from text prompts. Loss 15.5 → 3.16.
- Activation injection mechanism: hooks the embedding layer's forward pass to surgically overwrite the `<ACT_TOKEN>` row with `alpha · h_ℓ`. Gemma 4 specifically requires this (can't pass both `input_ids` and `inputs_embeds`, and the model verifies any `inputs_embeds` matches the embedding table for reverse-lookup of per-layer-inputs).
- inject_demo: extract h_16 from Gemma 4 for six prompts (factual/emotional/spatial/...), inject, sample strokes, render to PNG + animated MP4. Pulled to Mac at `findings/inject_demo/`.

### What was shortcircuited
- **Stage 2 AR supervised**: kicked off, ran for 20 steps (loss 5.85 → 3.21), killed early in favor of shipping the Day-1 visible artefact. The AR's `Linear(d, d)` head was barely trained; reconstruction faithfulness is correspondingly low.
- **Stage 3 AV GRPO RL**: not run. Without RL the AV's drawings aren't *faithful* to specific activations (i.e., different activations don't reliably produce systematically different drawings); they're whatever the SFT-bias plus the activation perturbation gives.
- **Per-layer sweep**: only layer 16 trained. The other 7 anchor layers (3, 8, 12, 20, 24, 29, 34) deferred to Day 2+.

### Insight surfaced during the conversation (worth recording)

When asked whether the AV would "just learn to write letters" in Stage 3 RL, my initial response was that "AR can't read letters → reward would drop". **That was wrong.** AR's backbone is full pretrained Gemma 4 E2B including the vision encoder, which can OCR. If the AV writes `cat` as strokes, the AR's vision encoder will represent the image similarly to text "cat" → reconstruction succeeds → reward flows → policy gradient reinforces letter writing.

**The actual arbiter between "sketch the concept" and "write the concept" is the KL coefficient β in Stage 3:**
- Large β: AV pulled toward Stage-1 SFT prior (QuickDraw sketches). Output: concept depictions.
- Small β: AV free to find the highest-bandwidth encoding. Output: probably letter writing (if stroke resolution allows it).

Either outcome is publishable. The first is "visual lens on residual stream", the second is "model verbalises its activations via motor channel". Need to update docs 02/05/07 to reflect this (done in this same commit).

### Strategic pivot (mid-session)

After running Day-1 + early Day-2 (Stage 2 finished, Stage 3 GRPO in flight), I gave the user an honest assessment that the **architecture is sound but the training recipe needs work**. Five binding constraints, in priority order:

1. **Alpha (injection scale) untuned.** Activation norm ~70 vs embedding norm ~10 → 7× magnitude mismatch.
2. **AR severely undertrained** (~400 (drawing, h) pairs total).
3. **AR's training data has bad semantics** — drawings generated from arbitrary text snippets, not from concept captions.
4. **GRPO step count is ~1% of NLA's** (we did 100 steps, they did ~10k).
5. **Cross-modal alignment may be weak** (Day-0 raw cosine delta ≈ 0).

**User-driven pivot**: instead of spreading to 8 layers, **make L16 genuinely work first**, then replicate. Roadmap updated in `07-execution-plan.md`:
- **Phase A**: get L16 to FVE ≥ 0.3 with visible per-activation drawings. Fix all 5 issues.
- **Phase B**: replicate to L8 and L24.
- **Phase C** (optional): full sweep, only if A+B succeed.

Recipe lock-in is the gate between phases. Quality > quantity.
