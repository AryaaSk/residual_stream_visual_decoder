## Decisions made

- **Architecture**: NLA-style autoencoder with stroke output, AR uses Gemma 4's vision pathway. Decided in conversation.
- **Base model**: Gemma 4 E2B (smallest open variant, multimodal-input).
- **Stroke representation**: Cartesian stroke-5 with 128-bin quantisation of Δx, Δy (not polar — Cursive Transformer's choice was an outlier; Cartesian generalises better).
- **Layer-sharing strategy**: full per-layer (Option A) is the gold standard. Train 8 independent (AVℓ, ARℓ) pairs. Anthropic's released NLAs are also per-layer.
- **Anchor layers**: default `{3, 6, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 26}`, overridden by Day-0 ROI ranking.
- **Repo**: PUBLIC from day 1, building in public.
- **Backup**: Mac mirrors everything important. GCP holds raw datasets. No HF Hub.
